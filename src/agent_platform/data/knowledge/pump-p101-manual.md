# Boiler Feed Pump P-101 Maintenance Manual

Illustrative document written for this reference implementation. Values are examples, not vendor data.

## Preventive Maintenance

The quarterly inspection of P-101 is performed every 90 days using job plan JP-PUMP-90D. Bearings are lubricated
every 2000 running hours with lithium-complex grease, 40 grams per bearing.

## Condition Monitoring Limits

Drive-end bearing vibration must stay below 4.5 mm/s RMS. Between 4.5 and 7.1 mm/s an inspection must be scheduled
within one week. Above 7.1 mm/s switch to standby pump P-102 and take P-101 out of service.

## Mechanical Seal Replacement

Seal kit SEAL-KIT-40 is required. Apply lock-out tag-out procedure LOTO-07 before work starts. The replacement takes
approximately 4 hours for one mechanical technician.
