# Lock-Out Tag-Out Procedure LOTO-07

Illustrative safety procedure written for this reference implementation.

## Steps

Notify the control room and obtain a permit to work. Stop the equipment and open the motor isolator. Every
technician applies a personal lock and a danger tag. Close and lock the suction and discharge valves. Verify zero
energy by attempting a start from the local panel.

## Removal

Locks are removed only by the person who applied them, or with written approval of the maintenance manager.
